# LinkedIn Post: UniTamil — When Technology Meets the Soul of Tamil

**Image**: /assets/images/headers/2026-02-15-unitamil.jpg

## Post Text

Tamil is not just a language. It is the first sound many of us heard as children. It is the script that carried our stories long before screens existed. 🧬❤️

Yet today, in the digital age, it faces a quieter danger — **being unreadable**.

Open an old Tamil book PDF. You can see the words, but you cannot search them, copy them, or pass them easily to the next generation. Thousands of Tamil books live today as **silent images**, locked inside legacy fonts like Bamini and TAB.

"𝘐𝘧 𝘮𝘺 𝘦𝘺𝘦𝘴 𝘤𝘢𝘯 𝘳𝘦𝘢𝘥 𝘛𝘢𝘮𝘪𝘭, 𝘸𝘩𝘺 𝘤𝘢𝘯’𝘵 𝘮𝘺 𝘤𝘰𝘮𝘱𝘶𝘵𝘦𝘳?"

That question led to 𝗨𝗻𝗶𝗧𝗮𝗺𝗶𝗹—an open-source initiative born from emotion, not just code. It's about:
✅ Letting old Tamil books speak again through OCR.
✅ Bridging the gap between legacy formats and Unicode.
✅ Ensuring Tamil exists freely for AI, search engines, and future tools.
✅ Keeping it offline, private, and dignified.

Unicode is not a technical upgrade; it is a **survival bridge**. Without it, a language slowly disappears from digital relevance.

UniTamil is my quiet promise to my heritage: "You carried us for centuries. Now we will carry you forward."
